02
Subscriptions

No refunds are issued on subscription payments already processed (Observer / Analyst / Sovereign). Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time through your account or by contacting us — cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and no further charges will be made.

If Faro Protocol is unable to deliver a report due to satellite data unavailability, Copernicus service outages, or internal processing failures, a full refund or equivalent report credit will be issued at the client's discretion. 04

Refund requests must be submitted to contact@faroprotocol.com within the eligible window, including your order number, the date of purchase, and a description of the issue. Requests submitted after the eligible period will not be considered.

05
Processing

Approved refunds are processed by Paddle within 5–10 business days to the original payment method. Faro Protocol does not handle refund disbursements directly. Paddle's refund timeline applies once the request has been approved and submitted by Faro Protocol.
